About

Built by someone who has seen the paperwork pile up.

Prapanch KokkalemadaFounder

I worked in commercial tire distribution at Ward Tires in Calgary. The work on the trucks was quick. The paper that followed it was not.

I coordinated sales and warehouse operations, then looked after fleet accounts. I saw unit numbers written on scraps of paper, POs chased by phone, and invoices coming back from fleets weeks after the job was done. Good technicians were losing their evenings to typing.

TreadFleet is the office help I wanted our customers to have: something that listens to the tech at the truck and does the paperwork properly, in English or French, without anyone learning a complicated system.

How we build · three rules

The rules TreadFleet won't break.

1

You approve everything

No work order, invoice or schedule goes to a fleet without the owner's tap. Every change is logged.

2

A record, not a guess

Prices come from your list, tax is calculated, and torque is stored exactly as your tech recorded it. TreadFleet never invents a value.

3

Your data stays yours, in Canada

Hosted in Canada, built for PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25. Export your jobs, invoices and records whenever you want.

Where we are

Early, and building it with the crews who'll use it.

TreadFleet is in development. The first version is being built with a small group of pilot crews in Ontario, Alberta and Quebec, using their real work orders, price lists and fleet rules.
