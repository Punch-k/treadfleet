Compare · what changes

The same road call, before and after.

Today

  1. Fleet calls your cell; details written on a scrap of paper
  2. Tech fills a paper work order with gloves off
  3. Torque value written down, if at all
  4. Evening: type it all into the invoice, look up prices and tax
  5. Fleet rejects it: PO missing. Start again

With TreadFleet

  1. The text becomes a job with fleet, unit and PO
  2. Tech records a 40-second voice note and three photos
  3. Torque saved per wheel; re-torque reminder sent
  4. Invoice drafted from your price list, tax calculated
  5. You tap approve. The fleet has it the same day
