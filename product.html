How it works · four steps

Four jobs off your desk

01 · INTAKE

Requests sorted, jobs created

Calls, texts, emails and WhatsApp from fleets land in one inbox. Each becomes a job with the right fleet, unit and PO.

02 · DISPATCH

The right tech, the right tire

Road calls slot in around yard checks: the closest tech with the right size on the truck. You confirm with one click.

03 · RECORD

Every wheel on record

Torque value, pattern, time and tech for every wheel, with re-torque reminders sent on their own.

04 · BILL

Invoiced before you're home

Work done versus quote, extras flagged, provincial tax applied, PO on every line. Approve and it's sent.
