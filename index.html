What it does · three jobs off your desk

Built around the commercial tire job, not a car-shop counter.

Recording · 0:38

01 · Voice notes

Talk with your gloves on.

Your tech presses one button and says what they did, in English or French. TreadFleet picks out the unit, wheel position, tread depth, tire size, torque and labour, and shows the words each line came from.

  • Works offline at the side of the highway
  • Photos attach to the right wheel
  • Learns your sizes, brands and fleets
How voice notes work
Re-torque reminder sent · 80 km

02 · Torque records

Every wheel on record.

Torque value, pattern, technician and time are saved with the job for every wheel. The driver gets a re-torque reminder after the first 50 to 100 miles, and you can pull the record in seconds if anyone asks.

  • Stored exactly as recorded, never filled in for you
  • Attached to the invoice the fleet receives
  • Exportable for audits and insurers
See the torque log
PO 77812 matched · unit 4521

03 · Fleet invoices

Invoices fleets don't send back.

The invoice is drafted from the job, priced from your own list, with the fleet's PO and unit number on every line and the right provincial tax. You approve it on your phone and it goes out the same day.

  • Work order, torque record and photos attached
  • Each fleet's PO rules checked before sending
  • Exports to QuickBooks and CSV
