Sound familiar?

The truck is fixed. The paperwork isn't.

Commercial techs are the hardest people in the shop to hire. Their time shouldn't go to paperwork, yet these six things eat it every week.

01

Work orders on paper and phones

Unit numbers, positions and tread depths end up on a clipboard, in a text and in someone's memory. Anything not written down never gets billed.

02

Torque records you can't prove

In Ontario a wheel separation is an absolute-liability offence, with fines up to $50,000. When a wheel comes off, the first question is: where's the torque record?

03

Road calls wreck the plan

A blowout on the highway pulls a tech off the yard check, national-account jobs need re-keying into their portals, and the whole day is rearranged by phone.

04

Invoices go out days late

Fleets send back invoices with the wrong unit number or a missing PO. Every day an invoice sits is another day before a slow payer even starts the clock.

05

Stock nobody can see

Which sizes are on which truck? Casings sitting at the retreader? You find out when the tire you need isn't there.

06

Software built for car shops

Most tools assume a bay, a car and a customer at the counter, not a unit number, a fleet PO and a shoulder of the 401.

How it works

From the road call to the paid invoice

An operations assistant that does the office work around every job. It prepares; you approve.

01 · CAPTURE

Requests and notes

Calls, texts and emails from fleets become jobs. At the truck, the tech speaks and snaps photos; tread depths, positions and parts are picked out.

02 · PLAN

Dispatch

Road calls slot in around planned yard checks, matching the closest tech with the right skills and the right tire on the truck.

03 · RECORD

Work order and torque log

Every wheel, every torque value, every casing, recorded per unit and position. Re-torque reminders go out on their own.

04 · BILL

Invoice and payment

The invoice is drafted from the work done, with the fleet's PO and provincial tax, ready before you're back at the shop.

FAQ

Questions crews ask

Is this another app my techs have to learn?

No. Techs press record and talk, the way they'd leave a voice message. Photos attach to the job. Everything else happens in the background.

Does it work for commercial truck tires, not just cars?

That's the whole point: unit numbers, steer, drive and trailer positions, 32nds, casings, retreads, torque and re-torque, fleet POs.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing goes out without your approval. You fix it in seconds, and it remembers the correction for next time. Prices always come from your own list.

Can it handle road calls and planned yard checks?

Yes. Road calls get slotted in around planned work, and the dispatch board shows who is closest with the right tire on the truck.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Invoices can be exported for your bookkeeper. Direct connections to common accounting tools are on the pilot roadmap; tell us which one you use.

Is it available in French?

Yes. The app, voice notes and invoices work in English and French.

What does it cost?

We're in the pilot phase. Pilot crews get lasting special pricing and are the first to know the final price.

Where is my data stored?

Hosting in Canada is planned for launch, built for PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25. You can export your data at any time.
