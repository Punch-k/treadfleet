Why you're still at the desk at 9 pm

The tools are put away. The paperwork isn't.

Commercial techs are the hardest people in the shop to hire. Their hours should go to trucks, not typing. These are the seven things that eat them.

01

Work orders scattered across clipboards and phones

Unit numbers, positions and tread depths live on paper, in a text, in someone's head. What isn't written down never gets billed.

One Toronto-area shop estimated $20–30k a year lost to missing paperwork
02

Torque records you can't prove

When a wheel comes off, the first question is: where's the torque record? In Ontario a wheel separation is an absolute-liability offence.

Fines $2,000–$50,000 (Ontario)
03

Road calls blow up the plan

A blowout on the 401 pulls a tech off the yard check, and the day gets rearranged by phone.

04

Invoices bounce back

Wrong unit number, missing PO. Every rejected invoice restarts the clock on a slow payer.

05

Stock nobody can see

Which sizes are on which truck? Casings at the retreader? You find out when the tire isn't there.

06

Another tool that doesn't talk to the others

Setup, training, five apps that don't connect. You learned the trade, not IT.

07

Software built for car shops

Bays, cars and a customer at the counter, not a unit number, a fleet PO and the shoulder of the highway.

What TreadFleet is

Not another app to feed. A digital dispatcher that does the office work around every job.

  • 🎙
    It listens like an experienced service writer. Your techs talk; it picks out units, positions, 32nds, parts and torque.
  • 🧾
    It prepares, you decide. Work orders, invoices and schedules wait for your one-tap approval.
  • 🧠
    It learns your shop. Your prices, brands, each fleet's PO rules. Every correction sharpens the next draft.
From the call to the paid invoice

Four jobs it takes off your desk

01 · INTAKE

Requests sorted, jobs created

Calls, texts, emails and WhatsApp from fleets land in one inbox. Each becomes a job with the right fleet, unit and PO.

02 · DISPATCH

The right tech, the right tire

Road calls slot in around yard checks: closest tech, right skills, the size on the truck. You confirm with one click.

03 · RECORD

Every wheel on record

Torque value, pattern, time and tech for every wheel, with re-torque reminders sent on their own.

04 · BILL

Invoiced before you're home

Quote versus work done, extras flagged, provincial tax applied, PO on every line. Approve and it's sent.

Is this another app my techs have to learn?

No. Techs press record and talk, like leaving a voice message. Photos attach to the job. Everything else happens in the background.

Does it work for commercial truck tires, not just cars?

That's the point: unit numbers, steer, drive and trailer positions, 32nds, casings, retreads, torque and re-torque, fleet POs.

What if it gets something wrong?

Nothing goes out without your approval. Fix it in seconds and it remembers the correction. Prices always come from your own list.

Can it handle road calls and planned yard checks?

Yes. Road calls slot in around planned work, and dispatch shows who is closest with the right tire on the truck.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Invoices export for your bookkeeper. Direct connections are on the pilot roadmap; tell us which tool you use.

What does it cost?

We're in the pilot phase. Pilot crews get lasting special pricing and are first to know the final price.
