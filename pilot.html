Questions

Before you apply

Do my techs have to learn a new app?

Barely. They press record and talk, like leaving a voice message, and take photos. Everything else happens in the office view.

Is it built for truck tires, or adapted from car-shop software?

Built for commercial work from the start: unit numbers, steer, drive and trailer positions, 32nds, casings, retreads, torque and re-torque, fleet POs.

What if it hears something wrong?

Every line shows the words it came from, unclear values are flagged, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Fix it once and it remembers.

Does it work with my accounting software?

Version one exports to QuickBooks Online and CSV. Tell us what you use; direct connections are next on the list.

Where is my data stored?

In Canada (Central region). Only your company can see your jobs, and you can export or delete them at any time.

What will it cost after the pilot?

Pricing will be a monthly plan per business, set with pilot crews. Pilot crews keep 30% off for their first paid year.
